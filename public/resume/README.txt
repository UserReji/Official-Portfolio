Place your resume PDF here with the filename:

  your-name-cv.pdf

Then update "resumeUrl" in src/lib/site.ts to match:

  resumeUrl: "/resume/your-name-cv.pdf",

The resume viewer on /resume will automatically detect and display it.
You can export your resume as PDF from:
  - Canva (free templates at canva.com)
  - Google Docs (File > Download > PDF)
  - Microsoft Word (File > Export > PDF)
